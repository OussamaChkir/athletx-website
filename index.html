Plan. Train. Track.

One workout planner, from your first plan to your last rep

Athletx keeps planning, training and tracking in a handful of simple screens, so you spend your energy on the workout instead of the app.

01 · Smart Plan

A weekly plan built around your muscles and your time

Choose the muscle groups you want to train and how long you have. Athletx shapes each session around both, so a busy day still gets a workout that fits.

  • Pick your focus muscles and a session length range
  • Follow a plan that progresses week by week, with each phase labelled
  • See today's workout at a glance: exercises, estimated time and calories, plus sets, reps and rest for every move

02 · Workout library

Prepared workouts for the days you'd rather not plan

Browse ready-made workouts, open a program, and see every training day and exercise before you commit to it.

  • Search and filter the library to find a program that fits your week
  • Preview each day of a program and the exercises inside it
  • On the free version, unlock a prepared workout for 24 hours by watching a rewarded ad. Premium unlocks them all

03 · Live workout

Log every set without breaking your flow

Enter reps and weight as you go, rest with a built-in timer, and open an exercise guide whenever you want a quick refresher on form.

  • Record reps and weight for each set in kilograms or pounds
  • Use the rest timer so recovery between sets stays consistent
  • Open an exercise for step-by-step instructions, the equipment you need, and the primary and secondary muscles it works

04 · Progress

See the work add up, week after week

Every finished workout is saved to your history, so you can look back at what you lifted and how your body weight is trending.

  • Review completed workouts with their sets, reps and weights
  • Log body weight entries and follow the trend over time
  • Get an estimated BMI that follows World Health Organization and CDC categories, with sources one tap away

How it works

From first launch to first workout in four steps

Athletx asks a few quick questions, builds your plan, and gets out of the way.

  1. 1

    Tell Athletx about you

    Answer a few short questions about your goal, how many days you can train, your equipment and the muscles you want to focus on.

  2. 2

    Get your Smart Plan

    Athletx builds a week-by-week plan around your answers and the time you have for each session.

  3. 3

    Train and log

    Follow the session, record reps and weight for each set, and use the rest timer between sets. Offline is fine.

  4. 4

    Review and adjust

    Check your history and body weight trend, then change your plan as your goals and schedule change.

FAQ

Questions about Athletx, answered

Is Athletx free?

Yes. Athletx is free to download and use. The free version shows ads, and Athletx Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes ads, unlocks unlimited custom plans and unlocks all prepared workouts.

Do I need an account to use Athletx?

No. Athletx does not require an account or a sign-up. Your plans, workouts and history are stored on your device.

Does Athletx work offline?

Yes. Your plans and workouts are stored on your device, so you can train without an internet connection. Features that need the internet, such as showing ads or making a purchase, need a connection.

How does the Smart Plan work?

You choose the muscle groups you want to train and how long you have for each session. Athletx then builds your workouts with sets, reps and rest times, and the plan progresses week by week.

What is a rewarded ad?

On the free version, you can watch a rewarded ad to unlock one prepared workout for 24 hours. Athletx Premium unlocks all prepared workouts and removes ads.

How do I cancel Athletx Premium?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID account settings. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can also restore a previous purchase from the Premium screen in the app.

Is the BMI estimate a medical diagnosis?

No. The BMI shown in Athletx is a general screening estimate calculated on your device, using standard categories published by public health authorities such as the World Health Organization and the CDC. It is not a diagnosis and it is not medical advice.

Which devices does Athletx support?

Athletx is available for iPhone on the App Store.

Does Athletx sell my data?

No. We do not sell your personal data. Your workout data stays on your device, and the third-party services used for ads and subscriptions handle limited information under their own privacy policies.
